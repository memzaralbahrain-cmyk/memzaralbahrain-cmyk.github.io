Privacy Policy

App: Ledger Book 53
Last updated: September 2026

Summary

Ledger Book 53 does not collect, upload, sell or share your personal or business data. All the information you enter stays on your own device.

Data you enter

Ledger names, vouchers, amounts, narrations and company details are stored only in your device's local browser storage. We do not have access to them and they are never sent to any server.

Backups

The backup file you download is saved on your device. You decide where to keep or share it. Clearing app or browser data, or uninstalling the app, can permanently delete your data, so please take regular backups.

Permissions

The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other sensitive permission. It uses no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Children

The app is a general business tool and is not directed at children.

Disclaimer

Ledger Book 53 is a record-keeping aid. Please verify important figures with a qualified accountant before using them for tax or legal filings.
